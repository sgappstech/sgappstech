Update April 2026

Yellow Dino Stickers v1.3 — new reactions added

Version 1.3 of Yellow Dino Stickers for WA is out, and it's the biggest update the pack has had since launch. Most of what changed came straight from requests submitted through our Contact page — this update exists because people told us what was missing.

New reactions

The update adds a batch of new dino expressions, including reactions aimed at group chats specifically — the ones people wanted when a plain thumbs-up sticker didn't feel like enough. We kept the same art style as the original set so the new stickers sit naturally alongside the ones already in your WhatsApp sticker tray.

Smaller download, faster loading

Alongside new content, v1.3 also focused on the app itself: the overall download size is smaller than previous versions, and the sticker pack loads faster the first time you open WhatsApp's sticker tray after installing. Neither change is visible in a screenshot, but they were the update's biggest engineering focus.

Why we ship updates this way

Every SG Apps Tech pack goes through the same checklist before an update ships: does it install quickly, does it work fully offline once set up, and does it respect the person using it. New stickers only make the cut if they don't compromise on the first two. You can read more about that approach on our About Us page.

Get the update

If Yellow Dino Stickers is already installed, updating through the Play Store will bring in the new reactions automatically. If you haven't tried it yet, visit the Yellow Dino Stickers app page for screenshots, the full feature list, and installation steps. Pair it with Bubu Panda Stickers or Animated Baby Stickers if you want more variety in your sticker tray.
